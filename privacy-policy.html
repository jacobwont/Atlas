Privacy Policy for Atlas

Last Updated: January 25, 2026

Introduction

Atlas ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use the Atlas app.

Information We Collect

Usage Data

We collect anonymous usage data to improve the Atlas experience. This includes:

  • Quiz performance and scores
  • Questions answered and topics completed
  • App features used and interaction patterns
  • Session duration and frequency of use
  • Device type and operating system version

This usage data is collected through analytics services and helps us understand how players engage with Atlas, which topics are most popular, and how we can improve the daily quiz experience.

Data Not Linked to You

All usage data collected is not linked to your personal identity. We do not collect:

  • Your name or email address (unless you create an account)
  • Contact information
  • Location data beyond country-level for global comparisons
  • Personal identifiers that can trace data back to you individually

Data Used to Track You

We use anonymized usage data for cross-app tracking purposes, which means:

  • Data may be shared with third-party analytics providers to understand app performance
  • This data is aggregated and cannot identify you personally
  • It helps us measure advertising effectiveness if we run promotional campaigns

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected usage data to:

  • Improve the Atlas quiz experience and question quality
  • Understand which geography topics are most engaging
  • Identify and fix technical issues
  • Analyze overall app performance and user engagement
  • Generate anonymized, aggregated statistics about global player performance

Score Comparison and Leaderboards

When you compare your scores with friends or view global player performance:

  • Scores are displayed in aggregate form
  • Individual performance data is anonymized
  • We do not share personally identifiable information with other players
  • Comparisons are based on quiz results only, not personal data

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share anonymized usage data with:

  • Analytics service providers (e.g., App Store analytics, third-party analytics tools)
  • Service providers who help us operate and improve Atlas

These third parties are required to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.

Data Retention

Usage data is retained for as long as necessary to improve the app and provide the daily quiz experience. Anonymized, aggregated data may be retained indefinitely for analytical purposes.
